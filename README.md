# GetSetEdit

## Overview

GetSetEdit is a digital marketing and content creation platform focused on helping businesses strengthen their online presence through professional content, social media management, and video editing services.

The platform showcases services, portfolio work, client information, and contact options, providing businesses with an effective way to connect with potential customers.

## Features

* Responsive and modern website design
* Service showcase section
* Social media marketing information
* Video editing and content creation services
* Contact and inquiry options
* Mobile-friendly user interface
* Fast and lightweight frontend implementation

## Tech Stack

* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript
* Bootstrap

## Project Objectives

* Build a professional online presence for GetSetEdit
* Present services in a clean and organized manner
* Improve client engagement and lead generation
* Deliver a responsive experience across devices

## Services Offered

* Social Media Management
* Video Editing
* Content Creation
* Brand Promotion
* Short-form Content (Reels & Shorts)
* Digital Marketing Support
